They lived in Alt Zedlisch (now Staré Sedliště in the Czech Republic), and were farm-lessees on the Heidlersches Gut farm.

Alt Zedlisch is located in the Bohemian region, roughly 7 kilometres southeast of the town of Tachov (Tachau).

In 1844, the prominent Bohemian balneologist and Marienbad spa doctor, Dr Karl Joseph Heidler, purchased the local lordship (Grundherrschaft) and chateau estate of Alt Zedlisch.Noble Nobility: For his widespread medical achievements and work treating tens of thousands of spa guests (including collaborating with Johann Wolfgang von Goethe), Emperor Franz Joseph I elevated him to the nobility in 1858, granting him the title Edler von Heilborn.Ownership Tenure: The manor, its agricultural farm lands (Gut), and the local castle remained the private property and a primary estate of the Heidler von Heilborn family for over a century, lasting up until the upheavals at the end of World War II in 1945.
