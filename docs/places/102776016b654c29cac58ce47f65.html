Maly Trostinec

A total of 16 trains carrying more than 15,000 people from Vienna, Königsberg, Theresienstadt, and Cologne arrived in Minsk between May and October 1942. In accordance with an order issued by the Chief of the Security Police and the SD, Reinhard Heydrich, the deportation victims were murdered immediately upon arrival. A pine woodland several kilometres from the estate of Maly Trostinec.

Of the approximately 8,700 Austrian Jews deported to Maly Trostinec — around 8,500 deported from Vienna and 136 from the Theresienstadt ghetto — only 17 known survivors remained.
