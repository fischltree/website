Theresienstadt

Terezin in Czech and Theresienstadt in German is a town in the Litoměřice District of the Ústí nad Labem Region of the Czech Republic.

Founded in 1780, Terezín is a former military fortress composed of the citadel and adjacent walled garrison town. The town centre is well preserved and is protected as an urban monument reservation. Terezín is infamously known as the location of the Nazis' Theresienstadt Ghetto. In the Family tree the reference to Terezin is to the town and as Theresienstadt for the Ghetto.
